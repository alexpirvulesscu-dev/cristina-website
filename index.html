The method

Precision you can see
before you decide.

  1. Scan

    The whole face first

    No plan starts at a tooth. It starts with how your face moves, rests and smiles — because that is where dental work either belongs, or shows.

  2. Measure

    Proportions, not guesses

    Symmetry, lip line, the width of each tooth against the next — measured and mapped before anything is touched. You see the same data I do.

  3. Plan

    Tooth by tooth

    Each tooth is its own decision: keep, whiten, reshape, restore. The most conservative option that achieves the result always wins.

Her story

GlasgowLondonBucharest

My approach to dentistry was shaped across different places, each adding something important to the way I practise today.

Glasgow gave me the discipline: rigorous standards, meticulous documentation, and a deep respect for healthy tooth structure. London refined the aesthetic side of my work, years spent planning smiles where proportion, restraint and detail mattered.

Coming back to Bucharest felt like the natural next chapter, bringing together everything I had learned into a way of working that feels entirely my own.

What I brought with me was not a foreign idea of dentistry, but a set of principles I believe in deeply: careful diagnosis, clear planning, informed consent, and a conservative approach by default.

The goal is never to do more than necessary. It is to understand what will genuinely improve a smile, protect what is already healthy, and make every decision with the patient.
