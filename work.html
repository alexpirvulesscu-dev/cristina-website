Case — Invisalign and full-mouth rehabilitation

Taking time to preserve her teeth

We began by treating decay, improving gum health and strengthening weakened teeth with composite restorations. Three and a half years of Invisalign brought her teeth into better alignment, allowing more conservative preparation for zirconia crowns and helping us avoid elective root canal treatment. Her full-mouth rehabilitation was completed over four years, with no root canal treatment needed and a protective splint provided to help protect against grinding.

  • Dental health stabilised before Invisalign
  • Teeth aligned to preserve more natural tooth structure during crown preparation
  • Four years overall, with no root canal treatment needed
  • Protective splint provided for grinding
