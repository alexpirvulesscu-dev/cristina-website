Who we are

This website is run by Dr. Cristina Pirvulescu, a dentist who works under the name Cristina Pirvulescu Dental Aesthetics. She decides how the details you send through this website are used, which makes her the “controller” in data-protection law. For any privacy question, write to info@cpdentalaesthetics.com.

What we collect

  • The contact form. Your name, email address and message, and the time you sent it. Your message may mention your teeth or your health, which is sensitive information. Please keep it short. We will go through your medical history with you at your consultation.
  • Visits to this website. Our web host records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address and the page you asked for, to keep the site running and secure.
  • WhatsApp and email. If you write to us there instead, we receive what you send. WhatsApp and your email provider handle it under their own policies.

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to you and, if you wish, to arrange a consultation. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. As your message may include health information, this is also your explicit consent for us to read it for that purpose. You can withdraw it at any time. Visit logs are kept to protect the website, which is our legitimate interest.

We do not sell your details or use them for marketing.

Who receives it

Service providers that help us run this website and deliver your message (web hosting, the form tool and email). If you ask for a consultation, we share your name, contact details and reason for your visit with the dental clinic where you will be seen, so they can book you in.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, limit or object to how we use them, or receive a copy in a portable form. You can withdraw your consent at any time. We reply within one month. Write to info@cpdentalaesthetics.com.

Complaints

You can complain to the Romanian data-protection authority, ANSPDCP. If you are in the United Kingdom, you can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

If we change this notice, we will update this page and the date at the top.
